# 7/17–7/19 承先啟後學習套件

本套件承接 [7/14 prerequisite interface](../2026-07-14/00_今日學習套件.md)，但不假定該次紀錄已完成，也不把 RN 當作全新定理重教。7/14 已負責 $L^p$ object、bounded tests、density generation 與 RN boundary；本套件只檢查這些介面能否被立即調用。這裡有三條相互配合但不可混成單一因果鏈的介面：

$$
\begin{aligned}
Y\in L^1(\mathbb P)
&\Longrightarrow
\mathbb E[Y]\text{ is finite and }Y\mathbb P\text{ is finite signed},\\
X\longmapsto\mu_X=X_\#\mathbb P
&\Longrightarrow
\mathbb E[\varphi(X)]=\int\varphi\,d\mu_X,\\
\mathbb P_B\text{ and finite partitions}
&\Longrightarrow
\text{Bayes and finite-partition conditional expectation}.
\end{aligned}
$$

The first line is the $L^p$ admissibility gate, the second is the pushforward representation, and the third is normalized restriction. The RN numerator from the first line returns as a representation engine on 7/26; it is not an intermediate definition of a law or of expectation.
