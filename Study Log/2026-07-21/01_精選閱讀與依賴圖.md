# 精選閱讀與依賴圖

## 7/21 — Product objects and Tonelli

先用 10 分鐘閉卷重建以下 objects。

Let $\mathcal R$ be the ring of finite disjoint unions of measurable rectangles. Define

$$
m_0\!\left(\bigsqcup_{i=1}^r A_i\times B_i\right)
=
\sum_{i=1}^r\mu(A_i)\nu(B_i).
$$

接著標出 construction boundary：

$$
\text{rectangle premeasure}
\to
\text{Carathéodory extension}
\to
\sigma\text{-finite uniqueness}.
$$

標出 well-definedness、countable additivity、extension 與 $\sigma$-finite uniqueness 分別進入何處。不重證 extension theorem，也不展開 outer-measure 細節。再閱讀 [Product Measures Cours](../../35_Product-Measures-and-Transformations/01_Cours/Cours.md) §§1–3，並完成正式 [Product Measures TD](../../35_Product-Measures-and-Transformations/02_TD/TD%2001%20—%20Product%20Measures,%20Iterated%20Integrals,%20and%20Transformations.md) Exercise A。必須重建：

$$
\text{rectangles}
\to
\mathcal A\otimes\mathcal B,
\qquad
f\ge0
\xrightarrow{\text{Tonelli}}
\text{iterated integrals in }[0,+\infty].
$$

本套件 Exercise 0 只保存 assignment pointer，不是第二題。

## 7/22 — Fubini and failure

閱讀 Product Measures Cours §4，並完成正式 Product Measures TD Exercise 0。必須區分：

$$
\text{positivity}
\quad\text{versus}\quad
\int|f|<+\infty.
$$

不要把反例摘要成「the series is conditionally convergent」。必須實際計算兩個 iterated sums 與 absolute sum。

本套件 Exercise 1 只保存 assignment pointer，不重複題目。

## 7/24 — Joint law and marginalization

$$
\mu_{X,Z}
\xrightarrow{\pi_X}

$$

The first arrow is a pushforward. The second is an RN representation. Tonelli computes the density.
