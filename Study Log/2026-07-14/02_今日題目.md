# 7/14 Problem Set — $L^p$ Interfaces and RN Consumers

The formal $L^p$ TD is the unique source for the assigned canonical exercises. This packet contains only the probability-space synthesis and the July consumer map.

## Exercise 0 — Reconstructing the probability-space $L^p$ interface

Let $(\Omega,\mathcal F,\mathbb P)$ be a probability space.

1. Define $L^p(\mathbb P)$ as a quotient space. State the equivalence relation and explain why the ambient measure is part of the object.
2. State

   $$
   L^\infty(\mathbb P)
   \subseteq
   L^2(\mathbb P)
   \subseteq
   L^1(\mathbb P),
   $$

   with the corresponding norm inequalities. Identify exactly where the assumption $\mathbb P(\Omega)=1$ enters. Do not re-prove the embedding here; that proof belongs to Canonical assignment A.
3. Translate $X\in L^p(\mathbb P)$ into a statement about the $p$-th absolute moment of $X$.
4. State the estimates which justify

   $$
   Y\in L^1,\ Z\in L^\infty
   \Longrightarrow
   YZ\in L^1,
   $$

   and

   $$
   U,V\in L^2
   \Longrightarrow
   UV\in L^1.
   $$

   Name the theorem used in each case. The endpoint proof and the $L^2$ proof belong respectively to Canonical assignment B and the formal $L^2$ Cours.

5. Assign each of the spaces $L^1$, $L^\infty$ and $L^2$ to its July consumer: expectation／RN numerator, bounded tests, or conditional second moments.

Limit: 15 minutes. This is an object-and-role reconstruction; proof responsibility lies in the canonical assignments below. Full projection theory is not requested.

## Canonical assignment C — Density measures and change of measure

Complete formal $L^p$ TD 01 Exercise 3(3), (5) and Exercise 4(1).

At the end, recover

$$
f\in L^1(\mu)
\Longrightarrow
\nu_f=f\mu
\text{ is a finite signed measure},
$$

$$
\nu_f=\nu_g
\Longrightarrow
f=g
\qquad\mu\text{-almost everywhere},
$$

and

$$
\nu=h\mu
\Longrightarrow
\int\varphi\,d\nu
=
\int\varphi h\,d\mu
$$

in the correct integrability regime.

## Closed-book reconstruction

Complete:

$$
\boxed{Y\in L^q(\mathbb P),\ q>1}
\Longrightarrow
\boxed{Y\in L^1(\mathbb P)}
\Longrightarrow
\boxed{Y\mathbb P\text{ is finite signed}}
\Longrightarrow
\boxed{\text{RN representations after a reference measure is chosen}}.
$$

The first arrow is an $L^p$ embedding. The second is density generation. The third is not automatic until numerator, reference measure, absolute continuity and the RN regime have been identified.
