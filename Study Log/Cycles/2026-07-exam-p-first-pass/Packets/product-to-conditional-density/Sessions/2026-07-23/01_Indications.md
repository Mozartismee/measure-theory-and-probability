# Indications — Feuille J4c

只有在 copie 上記下真實停滯位置與第一個不能合法繼續的步驟後，才閱讀對應的一條 relance。每次只開一條；若 relance 提供了缺失的 object、theorem 或 representation，必須在 Bilan 記錄。

Problème I.6(b) 與 Problème II.5 沒有 indication。

## Relance I.1 — From rectangles to all measurable sets

Use two different closure structures. The class of sets whose sections are measurable is a $\sigma$-field. For the integral identity, use the class on which section-integral measurability and equality with the product measure hold simultaneously; probability measures make complement subtraction finite.

## Relance I.2 — From indicators to Tonelli

Pass first to a disjoint nonnegative simple function. For $s_n\uparrow f$, use monotone convergence once on each section, once on the product space, and once on the outer integral.

## Relance I.3 — Separated integrands

For measurability, examine $(u\wedge n)(v\wedge n)$. Establish the nonnegative identity before treating signs; in the signed regime, the object to test first is $|u(x)v(y)|$.

## Relance I.4 — Product law

Both independence and equality of measures can be tested on the same measurable rectangles. What changes between the two statements is only the language used for their common value.

## Relance I.5 — Minimum

For each $t\ge0$, identify the event $\{\min(X,Y)>t\}$ before integrating in $t$.

## Relance II.1 — Addition and convolution

Compute the inverse image $a^{-1}(\{n\})$. Independence enters only after the joint masses on that fibre have been written down.

## Relance II.2 — Dependence boundaries

Fixed marginals constrain row and column sums, not the interior coupling. For zero covariance, look for a deterministic nonlinear relation whose odd mixed moment vanishes by symmetry.
