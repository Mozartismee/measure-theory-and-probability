## Intended use

TD 5–6 are direct references for conditional-expectation construction, observed tests and conditioning calculations. The remaining sequence calibrates downstream probability culture and assessment morphology; it does not silently add Brownian motion, martingales or Markov chains to the current Exam P foundation.
