# Analyse fonctionnelle — ENS Paris DMA

## Identity and provenance

- Level: ENS/FIMFA M1.
- Cours: Isabelle Gallagher, academic year 2019–2020.
- TD/corrigé sequence: DMA, academic year 2018–2019.
- Official course plan: [Plan du cours d'Analyse Fonctionnelle 2019–2020](https://www.math.ens.psl.eu/~gallagher/planENS-AF-1920.php).
- Official archive: [Feuilles de TD et TP](https://www.math.ens.psl.eu/lenseignement/feuilles-de-td-et-tp/) and [Annales d'examen](https://www.math.ens.psl.eu/lenseignement/annales-dexamen/).

The 2019–2020 Cours and the 2018–2019 TD sequence are deliberately separated by year. They are adjacent authoritative corpora, not falsely presented as one synchronized edition.

## Cours

- [Isabelle Gallagher — *Analyse Fonctionnelle*](2019-2020/Cours-Analyse-Fonctionnelle.pdf), 135 pages: Fréchet, Banach and Hilbert spaces; Baire and Hahn–Banach; weak topologies; distributions; Fourier analysis; Sobolev spaces; spectral theory.

## Intended use

Use this sequence to calibrate theorem boundaries, functional-analytic representations, weak compactness arguments, endpoint failures and the compression of a full corrigé. Only the $L^p$, Hilbert and duality interfaces directly consumed by the project may enter the core route without a separate scope decision.
