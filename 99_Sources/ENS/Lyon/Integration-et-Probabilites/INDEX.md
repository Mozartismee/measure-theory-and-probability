# Intégration et probabilités — ENS de Lyon

## Identity and provenance

- Lecturer/source curator: Emmanuel Jacob.
- Official teaching page: [Emmanuel Jacob — Teaching](https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/emmanuel.jacob/Teaching.htm).
- Levels: L3 integration and probability; the 2012–2013 material is the second integration course.

This is an independent ENS corpus used to cross-check the Paris DMA morphology.

## Intégration 2, 2012–2013

- [TD 00–11, compiled](2012-2013/TD-00-a-11.pdf): convergence of measurable functions, Fubini, convolution, $L^p$–$L^q$ duality, change of variables, Lebesgue differentiation, Fourier analysis and ergodic theory.
