# Cours — Integration and Convergence

Let $(E,\mathcal A,\mu)$ be a measure space. This file supplies the integration machinery used by every later module.

## 1. Construction of the integral

### Nonnegative simple functions

For

$$
\varphi=
\sum_{k=1}^m a_k\mathbf1_{A_k},
\qquad
a_k\ge0,
$$

with pairwise disjoint $A_k\in\mathcal A$, define

$$
\int_E\varphi\,d\mu
=
\sum_{k=1}^m a_k\mu(A_k).
$$

The value is independent of the chosen disjoint representation.

### Nonnegative measurable functions

For measurable $f:E\to[0,+\infty]$, define

$$
\int_E f\,d\mu
=
\sup\left\{
\int_E\varphi\,d\mu:
0\le\varphi\le f,
\ \varphi\text{ simple}
\right\}.
$$

Equivalently, if $(\varphi_n)$ is any sequence of nonnegative simple functions such that

$$
\varphi_n\uparrow f,
$$

then

$$
\int_E f\,d\mu
=
\lim_{n\to\infty}\int_E\varphi_n\,d\mu.
$$

### Signed functions

For a measurable real-valued $f$, write

$$
f=f^+-f^-,
\qquad
|f|=f^++f^-.
$$

The function is integrable if

$$
\int_E|f|\,d\mu<+\infty.
$$

Then

$$
\int_Ef\,d\mu
=
\int_Ef^+\,d\mu
-
\int_Ef^-\,d\mu.
$$

Absolute integrability is what prevents the undefined expression $+\infty-\infty$.

## 2. Monotone convergence theorem

### Theorem — MCT

Let $(f_n)$ be nonnegative measurable functions such that

$$
f_n\uparrow f
\qquad
\mu\text{-a.e.}
$$

Then

$$
\int_Ef_n\,d\mu
\uparrow
\int_Ef\,d\mu.
$$

### Proof architecture

Monotonicity gives

$$
\lim_n\int_Ef_n\,d\mu
\le
\int_Ef\,d\mu.
$$

For the reverse inequality, let $0\le\varphi\le f$ be simple and fix $c\in(0,1)$. Set

$$
A_n=\{f_n\ge c\varphi\}.
$$

Then $A_n\uparrow E$ up to the set on which the convergence fails, and

$$
\int_Ef_n\,d\mu
\ge
c\int_{A_n}\varphi\,d\mu.
$$

Passing to the limit gives

$$
\lim_n\int_Ef_n\,d\mu
\ge
c\int_E\varphi\,d\mu.
$$

Let $c\uparrow1$, then take the supremum over all simple $\varphi\le f$.

## 3. Fatou's lemma

### Theorem — Fatou

For nonnegative measurable functions $(f_n)$,

$$
\int_E\liminf_{n\to\infty}f_n\,d\mu
\le
\liminf_{n\to\infty}\int_Ef_n\,d\mu.
$$

### Reduction to MCT

Define

$$
g_n=inf_{k\ge n}f_k.
$$

Then

$$
g_n\uparrow\liminf_{n\to\infty}f_n
$$

and

$$
\int_Eg_n\,d\mu
\le
\inf_{k\ge n}\int_Ef_k\,d\mu.
$$

Apply MCT and pass to the limit.

Fatou is one-sided. Equality requires additional structure; optimism is not a convergence theorem.

## 4. Dominated convergence theorem

### Theorem — DCT

Let $(f_n)$ be measurable and suppose

$$
f_n\longrightarrow f
\qquad
\mu\text{-a.e.}
$$

Assume that there exists $g\in L^1(\mu)$ such that

$$
|f_n|\le g
\qquad
\mu\text{-a.e. for every }n.
$$

Then $f\in L^1(\mu)$ and

$$
\int_E|f_n-f|\,d\mu\longrightarrow0.
$$

In particular,

$$
\int_Ef_n\,d\mu
\longrightarrow
\int_Ef\,d\mu.
$$

### Proof architecture

Since $|f|\le g$ almost everywhere, $f\in L^1$. Apply Fatou to the two nonnegative sequences

$$
g+f_n,
\qquad
g-f_n.
$$

The resulting inequalities give

$$
\int_Ef\,d\mu
\le
\liminf_n\int_Ef_n\,d\mu
\le
\limsup_n\int_Ef_n\,d\mu
\le
\int_Ef\,d\mu.
$$

Applying the same argument to $|f_n-f|\le2g$ yields $L^1$ convergence.

## 5. Absolute continuity of the integral

### Proposition

If $f\in L^1(\mu)$, then for every $\varepsilon>0$ there exists $\delta>0$ such that

$$
\mu(A)<\delta
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\int_A|f|\,d\mu<\varepsilon.
$$

### Proof

Choose $M>0$ such that

$$
\int_{\{|f|>M\}}|f|\,d\mu<\frac\varepsilon2.
$$

Then

$$
\int_A|f|\,d\mu
\le
M\mu(A)
+
\int_{\{|f|>M\}}|f|\,d\mu.
$$

Take $\delta=\varepsilon/(2M)$.

## 6. Structural consequences

### Measures generated by densities

For measurable $f\ge0$,

$$
\nu(A)=\int_Af\,d\mu
$$

defines a positive measure. Countable additivity follows by applying MCT to the partial sums of indicators of disjoint sets.

### Nonnegative series

For measurable $f_n\ge0$,

$$
\int_E\sum_{n\ge1}f_n\,d\mu
=
\sum_{n\ge1}\int_Ef_n\,d\mu.
$$

This is MCT applied to the partial sums.

### Absolutely summable signed series

If

$$
\sum_{n\ge1}\|f_n\|_{L^1}<+\infty,
$$

then $\sum_n f_n$ converges absolutely almost everywhere and in $L^1$, and

$$
\int_E\sum_{n\ge1}f_n\,d\mu
=
\sum_{n\ge1}\int_Ef_n\,d\mu.
$$

## 7. Selection rule

| Available structure | Legitimate theorem |
| --- | --- |
| $0\le f_n\uparrow f$ | MCT |
| $f_n\ge0$ with no monotonicity | Fatou for a one-sided bound |
| $f_n\to f$ a.e. and $|f_n|\le g\in L^1$ | DCT |
| $\sum_n f_n$ with $f_n\ge0$ | MCT on partial sums |
| $\sum_n\|f_n\|_1<\infty$ | absolute convergence plus DCT/Tonelli |

Pointwise convergence alone gives no integral convergence. Uniform integrability is the later theory that replaces domination in broader regimes; it is not required in the present Exam P core.

## 8. Reconstruction

The following dependence must be recoverable:

$$
\text{simple integral}
\longrightarrow
\text{nonnegative integral}
\longrightarrow
\mathrm{MCT}
\longrightarrow
\mathrm{Fatou}
\longrightarrow
\mathrm{DCT}.
$$

The deployment chain is

$$
f\ge0
\longmapsto
f\mu
\longmapsto
\text{Radon–Nikodym representation}
\longmapsto
\text{conditional expectation}.
$$
